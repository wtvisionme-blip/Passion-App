# Passion-App

App for digital products creation.

## Claude Cowork Automation Templates

Six SOP templates for automating recurring work with Claude. Each template lives in `templates/` with full inputs, steps, and a copy-paste prompt.

| # | Template | Use Case |
|---|----------|----------|
| 1 | [Digital Cleanup](templates/01-digital-cleanup.md) | File management & organization |
| 2 | [Receipt-to-Spreadsheet](templates/02-receipt-to-spreadsheet.md) | Accounting & expense tracking |
| 3 | [Meeting Recap](templates/03-meeting-recap.md) | Project management |
| 4 | [Competitor Intelligence](templates/04-competitor-intelligence.md) | Market research |
| 5 | [Inbox Watchdog](templates/05-inbox-watchdog.md) | Email routine automation |
| 6 | [Content Repurposer](templates/06-content-repurposer.md) | Social media content |

### Quick Trigger Phrases
- `"Run Digital Cleanup on [FOLDER]"`
- `"Run Receipt Scan on [FOLDER WITH RECEIPTS]"`
- `"Run Meeting Recap on [MEETING FOLDER]"`
- `"Run Competitor Research on [INDUSTRY]"`
- `"Run Inbox Watchdog for [NAME] about [TOPIC]"`
- `"Run Content Repurposer on [FOLDER] for [PLATFORM]"`
