# AI Compliance Architect — Discovery Call Framework

Use this before every new client call. Work through each section in order.

---

## Section 1 — Their AI Footprint
- What AI tools are your employees currently using? (sanctioned AND unsanctioned)
- Are you building AI internally, buying it from vendors, or both?
- Who owns the AI decisions — IT, legal, operations, or a mix?
- Do you have a list of every AI system touching your data right now?

## Section 2 — Data & Privacy
- What kinds of data are you feeding into AI systems? (PII, financial, health, proprietary)
- Do you know where that data goes once it enters an AI tool?
- Are employees using personal ChatGPT accounts for work tasks?
- Do you have data retention or deletion policies that cover AI outputs?

## Section 3 — Regulatory Environment
- What industry are you in?
- Are you subject to GDPR, HIPAA, CCPA, SOC 2, or any other frameworks?
- Have regulators or auditors asked you about AI yet?
- Do you operate in the EU? (EU AI Act applies)

## Section 4 — Risk & Incidents
- Have you had any AI-related incidents — data leaks, biased outputs, hallucinations that caused problems?
- What's your biggest fear about AI going wrong in your organization?
- Do you have a process for employees to report AI issues?

## Section 5 — Current Policies & Governance
- Do you have an AI use policy right now?
- Who approves new AI tools before they're adopted?
- Do your vendor contracts address AI data usage?
- Do you do any AI model auditing or output monitoring?

## Section 6 — People & Culture
- How AI-literate is your team?
- Is leadership bought into AI governance or are they resistant?
- Do employees know what they're allowed and not allowed to do with AI?

## Section 7 — Budget & Decision-Making
- Who signs off on compliance investments?
- Have you budgeted for AI governance this year?
- Are you looking for a one-time assessment or ongoing compliance support?
- What does success look like for you in 6 months?

---

## The 3 Power Questions (Use If Time Is Short)
1. "What AI tools are in your org right now — including ones IT didn't approve?"
2. "Have you had any regulator, auditor, or board-level questions about AI yet?"
3. "Who would be accountable if an AI system caused a compliance violation tomorrow?"

---

## After the Call — Next Steps Checklist
- [ ] Document which AI tools they mentioned
- [ ] Note which regulations apply to their industry
- [ ] Identify their biggest risk area (data, governance, policy, culture)
- [ ] Flag if they have shadow AI usage (unsanctioned tools)
- [ ] Propose: one-time assessment OR ongoing retainer
